Questions and answers

Everything people ask before and after buying.

Are the phones unlocked?

Yes. Every phone is unlocked and works on any US carrier.

What do the grades mean?

Excellent looks like new with battery health of 90% or higher. Good has light signs of use with 85% or higher. Fair has clear wear such as scratches or scuffs, everything works, and battery health is 80% or higher.

How are phones tested?

Each phone is checked for battery, screen and touch, front and rear cameras, speakers and microphones, buttons and ports, and cellular and Wi-Fi before it ships.

What does the warranty cover?

Hardware faults for 12 months. If a phone develops one, we repair it, replace it, or refund you. It does not cover physical or liquid damage or unauthorised repairs.

How do returns work?

You have 30 days from delivery. Tell us through Customer service or Telegram that you want to return the phone, and we arrange the return. You get a full refund of the purchase price once we have checked the phone.

Is shipping free?

Yes, on every order. The estimated delivery date appears before you add a phone to your cart.

How do I pay?

At checkout you choose ATM card, bank transfer or cryptocurrency. After you place the order, the payment details appear in your dashboard under Payments. Pay, enter your transfer reference or transaction ID, then press I have made the payment.

Which network should I use for cryptocurrency?

Use exactly the network shown next to each wallet address in your dashboard. A transfer sent on the wrong network cannot be recovered.

Why can I not see payment details yet?

We send them from our admin dashboard after your order arrives. The page updates by itself, so keep your dashboard open or come back shortly. If it takes long, message us on Telegram.

How do I track my order?

Open your dashboard and tap Orders. You see each step from payment confirmation to delivery, and a Google map of the parcel and your delivery address once it is on its way.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Use Continue with Google on the sign-in page, or create an account with your email and a password.

How do I leave a review?

When your order shows Delivered, a review box appears on that order in your dashboard. Reviews are shown on the store after we approve them.

How do I contact you?

Use Customer service in your dashboard, or message us on Telegram at t.me/fairgradestore.
