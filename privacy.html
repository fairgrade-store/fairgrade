Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Fairgrade collects, why, and what choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name, and an optional profile photo.
  • Order details: the phones you order, your phone number, delivery address, payment method and payment reference or transaction ID.
  • Messages: what you send us through Customer service, and our replies.
  • Anonymous visit data: a random session ID, the pages you view, your device type (mobile or desktop), the website you came from, the phone models you look at, and whether you add to the cart.

2. How we use it

We use your information to create and manage your account, take and deliver your orders, verify payments, answer your messages, prevent fraud, and understand how the store is used so we can improve it.

3. Anonymous analytics

The visit data above is not linked to your name or email. It is stored with a random ID that lives in your browser only while the tab is open. We do not use advertising trackers.

4. Who can see your information

Only you and our store team can see your orders and messages. We do not sell your information. We use service providers to run the store: Google Firebase for accounts and our database, GitHub to host the website, Google Fonts for typefaces, Google sign-in if you choose it, and Google Maps when you open the delivery map. These providers process data on our behalf.

5. Payments

We do not collect or store your card number on this website. For card payments we send you a payment link or instructions. For bank transfers and cryptocurrency we store the reference or transaction ID that you give us so we can verify your payment.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to deliver your order, handle returns and warranty claims, and meet legal and accounting duties. You can ask us to delete your account and other personal information, and we will do so unless we must keep it by law.

7. Your choices

You can see and edit your name, phone number, address and photo on the Profile page of your dashboard. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time through Customer service in your dashboard.

8. Security

Your account is protected by your password, and our database rules mean customers can only see their own orders and messages. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you do not use elsewhere.

9. Children

Our store is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

11. Contact

For any privacy question, use Customer service in your dashboard, message us on Telegram at t.me/fairgradestore, or email fairgradestore@gmail.com.
