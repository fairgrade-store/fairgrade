About Fairgrade

Used iPhones, graded honestly.

Fairgrade sells used iPhones and matching gadgets. Every phone is tested, graded by how it looks and how its battery performs, and covered by a 12-month warranty. The condition you pick is the condition you get.

What we promise

Tested before it shipsWe check battery health, screen and touch, front and rear cameras, speakers and microphones, buttons and ports, and cellular and Wi-Fi.
Three clear gradesExcellent has no visible marks and battery of 90% or higher. Good has light signs of use and 85% or higher. Fair has clear wear, everything works, and battery of 80% or higher.
12-month warrantyHardware faults are covered. We repair, replace or refund.
30-day returnsChange your mind within 30 days and send it back for a refund.
Free shippingOn every order, with the delivery date shown before you add a phone to your cart.

How payment stays safe

After you place an order, our payment details appear inside your signed-in dashboard, under Payments. Only pay to the details shown there. We do not send bank details through email or chat apps, and we will never ask for your card number or password.

Follow your order

Your dashboard shows each step from payment confirmation to delivery, with a Google map of your parcel and delivery address. When your order is delivered you can leave a review, and reviews are shown on the store only after we approve them.
